How to enter a junior golf tournament in Japan

Most tournament pages and entry forms in Japan are written only in Japanese. This page explains how school grades work, who can enter, how entry usually works, and the words you will see.

Rules differ by tournament. Always read the official guidelines (要項) for the event you want to enter.

1. Check the school grade, not just the age

Japan’s school year starts on April 1. Many tournaments divide players by school grade (学年), which is decided by date of birth. For the 2026 school year (April 2026 to March 2027):

Other tournaments, such as U.S. Kids Golf, use age divisions. The guidelines say on which date the age is counted. If your child attends an international school and you are not sure which grade applies, ask the organizer before entering.

2. Check who can enter

  • Area conditions are common. Prefecture tournaments often require the player to live in (在住) or attend school in (在学) that prefecture.
  • Nationality. The guidelines we have checked for major organizers do not mention nationality. That is not a guarantee, so read the guidelines for each tournament.
  • Some series need a membership first. For example, JGA junior membership for JGA junior championships, JJGT membership for the JJGT tour, and school golf club registration for school federation (高ゴ連) tournaments.

3. How entry usually works

  1. Open the official page from our list and find the guidelines (要項), often a PDF.
  2. Check the entry period (申込期間). Some tournaments are first come, first served (先着順) and close early when full.
  3. Apply on the organizer’s web form or member page. Some still use fax or post. Forms often ask for your child’s name in katakana (フリガナ).
  4. Pay the entry fee (参加料), often by Japanese bank transfer (銀行振込) or at a convenience store. The course green fee (プレー費) may be separate.
  5. Check pairings and tee times (組合せ). They are usually posted on the official page before the tournament.

Tip: your browser’s translate function works on most official pages. For PDFs, a translation app with a camera mode helps.
